# Star table sources and simulation notes

The nine named personas are training simulations, not endorsements, measured player models, or claims about current real-world behavior. All stack ranges and numeric tendencies are game-design choices. Britney's short-stack persona follows the requested scenario; it does not assert that she always plays that way. ST Wang's sample is limited. Cash tables only; random seats without replacement, reproducible from the session seed.

Bot decisions use only their own hole cards and public actions. Baseline GTO advice is unchanged; it is not an exact solver best response to these personas.
